I made a whiteboard lecture explaining BrandMorph — the engine that re-skins PowerPoint decks in place: repaint the theme "paint tin", remap every hand-painted color in Lab space so each color keeps its role, keep text inside its box with real font metrics, and run twice with zero changes. Every change lands in a receipt; hostile decks (zip bombs, entity bombs) stop at the door. 43 tests; the committed demo deck ships 65 audited changes with fidelity preserved.
